The selected route and coastline data, including their adaptations, are offered here free of charge in machine-readable form. These are modern geographic data acquired on 3 November 2026. They do not establish how the road, buildings or traffic looked in the 1990s.

  • Source coordinates, identifiers, query and provenance
  • Selected coordinates used by the renderer
  • Projection, clipping, curve and geometry source
  • Vehicle motion source
  • Data notice and scope

The longitude and latitude values are projected into local metres around the first route coordinate. The route is densified at no more than four metres between controls and interpolated with a non-looping centripetal curve. The same curve supplies the road and vehicle pose. The coastline is clipped; a simple inland closure forms the land surface. The linked source provides the complete transformations.

Road widths, vertical levels and building forms are fitting assumptions. Surface designs and rendering code are original work, separate from the geographic database. This is an early coastal study, not a historical or surveyed reconstruction.
