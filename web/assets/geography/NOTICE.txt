The accompanying bari-coast.geojson contains the selected source route and
coastline coordinates, OSM way and node identifiers, acquisition details and
the source query. bari-data.js carries the same selected coordinate values
for the renderer. These geographic data and their adaptations remain under
ODbL 1.0. Rendering code and original surface designs are separate works.

The route uses ways 68558009, 1226173975 and 30009729. The clipped coastline
uses ways 316357763 and 442458193. A local projection converts longitude and
latitude to metres. A centripetal curve derived from the route is used for
both the road and vehicle pose. Its source deviation limit and measured
control-hull bound are exposed by the world module.

Modern geographic data do not establish 1990s traffic restrictions, street
furniture, building appearance or coastal works. Road width, vertical levels,
land closure and original building forms are fitting assumptions. The detailed
section around 760-980 metres is an unapproved visual proposal. Other buildings
remain massing. No Google geometry, photographs or remote textures are included.
